I've been finishing **Fresh Prints**, a fast social game built around one question: _AI or not?_

You get a card. Sometimes it's real, sometimes a machine made it. You call it, you score, and then you argue with your friends about how you possibly got that one wrong.

It's the closest to done any game of mine has been in a long time, and the one I'm most excited to put in people's hands. When it's ready, it'll live at [freshprints.gg](https://freshprints.gg).
