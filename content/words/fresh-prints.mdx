I've been finishing **Fresh Prints**, a daily challenge about telling real images from generated ones.

You get a fresh set every day. First you work out which ones a machine made, which is harder than it sounds and getting harder every month. Then, for the ones that are generated, you go a step further and guess the exact prompt behind them.

That second half is the part I keep coming back to. Calling an image fake is a yes or no. Naming the prompt makes you say out loud what you think you're looking at, and more often than not, writing it down is the thing that tells you whether you were right.

It's a phone game, so it's headed for the App Store and Google Play. Very soon, which is what I have been saying for a while now and would like to stop saying.

It's the closest to done any game of mine has been in a long time, and the one I'm most excited to put in people's hands. It'll live at [freshprints.gg](https://freshprints.gg), which for now is a signup box and my word for it.

More soon.
